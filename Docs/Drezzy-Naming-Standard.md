# Drezzy naming standard

Agreed at the team meeting on 4 November 2026. Every product follows one order:

**Brand + Model + Cut + Colour + Size**

| Part | Example | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand | Nike | Brand field |
| Model | Dunk | Model field. The product name becomes "Nike Dunk" |
| Cut or style | Low, Mid, High | Cuts field. Leave empty if the product has none |
| Colour | Black / White | Colours field |
| Size | 42 | Sizes field |

Shown to customers and staff as: **Nike Dunk Low · Black / White · Size 42**

## Rules

- Cuts, colours and sizes are not typed into the name. Each combination becomes its own variant with its own price and availability.
- Colourways are written with a slash and capitals: Black / White.
- Shoes use EU sizes as plain numbers: 42, not EU 42.
- Clothing uses XS, S, M, L, XL, 2XL, 3XL, 4XL, 5XL. The system turns "XXL" into "2XL" for you.
- Watches and caps use One size.
